The Aboriginal Health & Wellness Centre is an Indigenous-led group that provides health and healing for Indigenous people. They offer mental health support and cultural programs. These services help families heal from trauma and find wellness. They also provide the Indian Residential School (IRS) Resolution Health Support Program. This program offers culturally safe emotional and grief support for residential school survivors and their families as they deal with personal trauma and loss through one-on-one professional counselling and cultural healing by providing access to Elders and Knowledge Carriers who offer traditional healing ceremonies, teachings, and sharing circles. Additionally, community-based health support workers are available to listen, talk, and share safe spaces with participants throughout their healing journeys.

It specifically provides emotional support and clinical counselling to help individuals and families navigate the intense grief, loss, and trauma associated with the legacy of Indian Residential Schools and Day Schools, as well as the ongoing tragedy of Missing and Murdered Indigenous Women and Girls (MMIWG).

Giwiidabindimin Certificate Program – Responding to Trauma and Grief with Decolonized Practices

1-710 Westminster Avenue

Winnipeg, MB

R3G 1A3

1 877 353-3205

info@ctrinstitute.com

https://ctrinstitute.com/topic/certificate-program-in-responding-trauma-grief-indigenous/

The Crisis & Trauma Resource Institute offers the "Giwiidabindimin: Responding to Trauma and Grief with Decolonized Practices" certificate program and the Children's Grief and Bereavement Certificate Program. This five-day training program combines Indigenous cultural practices with Western clinical methods to support helpers, professionals, and community members working with trauma and loss. Co-facilitated by Indigenous and non-Indigenous trainers, the program teaches a "two-eyed seeing" approach to help participants understand the impacts of colonization, recognize historical trauma, and support collective, community-based healing through meaningful relationships and tradition.

Medicine Bear Counselling, Support, and Elder Services helps families of missing and murdered loved ones heal from trauma by offering a missing persons toolkit, individual, family, and crisis-counselling sessions, and weekly circle check-ins. They provide Elder support services, traditional ceremonies and teachings, training and workshops, referrals, advocacy, and mediation services for families, police, and justice officials. First Peoples Wellness Circle (FPWC)

857 Yellek Trail

North Bay, ON

P1B 8G5

1 717 313-4942

info@fpwc.ca

https://fpwc.ca/

The First Peoples Wellness Circle is an Indigenous-led group that provides mental health and trauma support for adults and youth. They offer a Mental Wellness and Crisis Response Team training based on Indigenous Knowledge to help workers support communities, alongside a Knowledge Exchange and Resource Sharing Hub providing toolkits and webinars for the mental wellness workforce. Through their "Gathering Our Knowledge Bundles" project, they provide a dedicated Grief and Loss Bundle containing informational tip sheets and guidance resources to help community helpers support individuals dealing with complex, multi-generational grief.

Hope for Wellness Helpline

1 855 242-3310

https://www.hopeforwellness.ca

The Hope for Wellness Helpline is available to all Indigenous people across Canada. Experienced and culturally competent counsellors are reachable by telephone and online ‘chat’ 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

The Missing and Murdered Indigenous Women and Girls Support Line

1 844 413-6649

https://www.mmiwg-ffada.ca/contact/

The MMIWG Crisis Line provides 24/7 immediate emotional assistance via a toll-free national crisis line (1-844-413-6649) for anyone affected by the loss of Indigenous women, girls, and Two-Spirit individuals (MMIWG2S+). The service offers crisis care in languages such as Cree and Inuktitut, as well as community-based emotional support workers providing trauma-informed listening, talking, and referrals. Additional services include professional mental health counselling by registered psychologists and social workers, and long-term health supports including assistance with travel costs to access cultural supports, ceremonies, prayer, Elder services, and traditional healing.
