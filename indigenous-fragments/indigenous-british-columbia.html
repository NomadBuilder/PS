The First Nations Health Authority supports the training of End-of-Life Doulas, who provide holistic cultural, emotional, and spiritual support to Indigenous individuals and families navigating the final journey to the Spirit World. These doulas assist with end-of-life planning, provide comfort measures, and act as supportive mediators for families to help them process their grief. They offer specific grief care by guiding families to turn fear and anger into healing prayers and traditional songs. This helps ease the heavy hearts of grieving relatives while ensuring their loved one passes peacefully surrounded by ancestral and community values.

The Round Lake Alcohol and Drug Treatment Society provides residential treatment for adults to recover from substance use. Indigenous

Expanded Trauma-Informed Health and Cultural Support Program

https://www.sac-isc.gc.ca/eng/1736168777040/1736168826573

The Expanded Trauma-Informed Health and Cultural Support Program provides cultural and emotional care for survivors and families. They offer unique services like Elder support, traditional healing, and counselling. This program helps families find wellness and healing from historical trauma. It specifically provides emotional support and clinical counselling to help individuals and families navigate the intense grief, loss, and trauma associated with the legacy of Indian Residential Schools and Day Schools, as well as the ongoing tragedy of Missing and Murdered Indigenous Women and Girls (MMIWG). Access to this service is through the Indigenous Services Canada regional office in each province.

First Peoples Wellness Circle (FPWC)

857 Yellek Trail

North Bay, ON

P1B 8G5

1 717 313-4942

info@fpwc.ca

https://fpwc.ca/

The First Peoples Wellness Circle is an Indigenous-led group that provides mental health and trauma support for adults and youth. They offer a Mental Wellness and Crisis Response Team training based on Indigenous Knowledge to help workers support communities, alongside a Knowledge Exchange and Resource Sharing Hub providing toolkits and webinars for the mental wellness workforce. Through their "Gathering Our Knowledge Bundles" project, they provide a dedicated Grief and Loss Bundle containing informational tip sheets and guidance resources to help community helpers support individuals dealing with complex, multi-generational grief.

Hope for Wellness Helpline

1 855 242-3310

https://www.hopeforwellness.ca

The Hope for Wellness Helpline is available to all Indigenous people across Canada. Experienced and culturally competent counsellors are reachable by telephone and online ‘chat’ 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

The Missing and Murdered Indigenous Women and Girls Support Line

1 844 413-6649

https://www.mmiwg-ffada.ca/contact/

The MMIWG Crisis Line provides 24/7 immediate emotional assistance via a toll-free national crisis line (1-844-413-6649) for anyone affected by the loss of Indigenous women, girls, and Two-Spirit individuals (MMIWG2S+). The service offers crisis care in languages such as Cree and Inuktitut, as well as community-based emotional support workers providing trauma-informed listening, talking, and referrals. Additional services include professional mental health counselling by registered psychologists and social workers, and long-term health supports including assistance with travel costs to access cultural supports, ceremonies, prayer, Elder services, and traditional healing.
