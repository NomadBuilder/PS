Alvin Hamilton Building 1783 Hamilton Street, Room 098

The Expanded Trauma-Informed Health and Cultural Support Program provides cultural and emotional care for survivors and families. They offer unique services like Elder support, traditional healing, and counselling. This program helps families find wellness and healing from historical trauma. It specifically provides emotional support and clinical counselling to help individuals and families navigate the intense grief, loss, and trauma associated with the legacy of Indian Residential Schools and Day Schools, as well as the ongoing tragedy of Missing and Murdered Indigenous Women and Girls (MMIWG).

Mental Wellness Supports for Métis Citizens – Crisis Line

310 20th Street East

Saskatoon, SK

S7K 0A7

1 855 671-5638

healthprograms@mns.work

https://metisnationsk.com/2023/08/08/mental-health-and-addictions-program/

The Métis Nation–Saskatchewan is an Indigenous-led group that provides culturally safe care and support. Their services include the Funeral and Bereavement Support Program, which offers financial assistance up to $2,500 per family unit for funeral expenses, travel, accommodation, and transportation of a deceased loved one. They also provide the Family Information Liaison Outreach (FILO) program, offering Métis-specific trauma and ongoing grief support for families of missing or lost loved ones, alongside Mental Wellness Support supplying culturally specific mental wellness and 24/7 crisis line support in English and French for adults, youth, and families.

Métis Nation of Saskatchewan

310 20th Street East

Saskatoon, SK

S7K 0A7

1 877 638-4775

https://metisnationsk.com

Métis Nation of Saskatchewan provides health, housing, and cultural care for Métis families. They offer unique programs like medical travel help, first-time home buyer grants, land-based healing, and the Funeral and Bereavement Support Program. This program provides registered citizens with up to $2,500 for eligible expenses, including direct payments to funeral homes, travel and accommodation reimbursement for immediate family, transporting a deceased loved one, and Métis sashes or urns upon request. These services help the community find wellness and stay connected to Métis heritage.

Métis Nation–Saskatchewan Funeral and Bereavement Support Program

310 20th Street East

Saskatoon, SK

S7K 0A7

1 877 638-4775

mnsfuneral@mns.ca

https://mns.ca/services-and-programs/applications/funeral-and-bereavement

The Métis Nation–Saskatchewan runs this program to help Métis citizens and families with funeral costs, providing up to $2,500 per family unit for eligible funeral expenses with direct payments to funeral homes. The program offers travel and accommodation reimbursement for immediate family members attending a service, financial assistance for transporting a deceased loved one back to their home community, and the provision of Métis sashes or urns upon request. Additionally, it connects grieving families directly to professional mental health supports, including free, specialized grief counseling and culturally safe therapeutic resources.

First Peoples Wellness Circle (FPWC)

857 Yellek Trail

North Bay, ON

P1B 8G5

1 717 313-4942

info@fpwc.ca

https://fpwc.ca/

The First Peoples Wellness Circle is an Indigenous-led group that provides mental health and trauma support for adults and youth. They offer a Mental Wellness and Crisis Response Team training based on Indigenous Knowledge to help workers support communities, alongside a Knowledge Exchange and Resource Sharing Hub providing toolkits and webinars for the mental wellness workforce. Through their "Gathering Our Knowledge Bundles" project, they provide a dedicated Grief and Loss Bundle containing informational tip sheets and guidance resources to help community helpers support individuals dealing with complex, multi-generational grief.

Hope for Wellness Helpline

1 855 242-3310

https://www.hopeforwellness.ca

The Hope for Wellness Helpline is available to all Indigenous people across Canada. Experienced and culturally competent counsellors are reachable by telephone and online ‘chat’ 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Talk 4 Healing

100 Anemki Drive, Suite 103,

Fort William First Nation, ON

P7J 1J4

https://www.beendigen.com/programs/talk4healing/

The Talk 4 Healing helpline provides 24/7 support for Indigenous women in fourteen different languages. They offer unique services like culturally safe talk, text, and chat options. This program helps women find healing and wellness by providing a safe space to share stories and find help. Their team provides immediate, non-judgmental crisis counselling and emotional support specifically tailored to help women navigate personal struggles, deep emotional distress, and the heavy burden of grief and loss.

The Missing and Murdered Indigenous Women and Girls Support Line

1 844 413-6649

https://www.mmiwg-ffada.ca/contact/

The MMIWG Crisis Line provides 24/7 immediate emotional assistance via a toll-free national crisis line (1-844-413-6649) for anyone affected by the loss of Indigenous women, girls, and Two-Spirit individuals (MMIWG2S+). The service offers crisis care in languages such as Cree and Inuktitut, as well as community-based emotional support workers providing trauma-informed listening, talking, and referrals. Additional services include professional mental health counselling by registered psychologists and social workers, and long-term health supports including assistance with travel costs to access cultural supports, ceremonies, prayer, Elder services, and traditional healing.
